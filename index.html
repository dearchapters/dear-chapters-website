Before you

A few answers to help you get started.

Is this a physical planner?＋

No. Dear Chapters is a digital product. No physical item will be shipped.

Which device can I use?＋

The planner is designed primarily for iPad landscape use with a compatible digital note-taking application.

Are all four editions different inside?＋

All four editions contain the same planning structure and features. The difference is the visual aesthetic and cover design.

What is included in the Trial Edition?＋

The Trial Edition covers December 2026 and January 2027. It offers a shorter introduction to the Dear Chapters planning experience.

Can I upgrade after purchasing the trial?＋

Yes. You can upgrade to the Full 2027 Edition for an additional RM44.90.

How will I receive my planner?＋

Your digital files and download instructions will be provided after purchase through the checkout platform.

Can I share or resell my planner?＋

Your purchase is for personal use only. The planner and its contents may not be redistributed, resold or shared.
