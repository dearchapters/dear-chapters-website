A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

What is a digital planner?

A digital planner is an electronic planning file designed to be used on a compatible tablet or device with a digital note-taking application.

What device can I use?

Dear Chapters is designed primarily for iPad landscape use. You will need a compatible digital note-taking application to import and use the planner.

Do I receive a physical planner?

No. Dear Chapters is a digital product. No physical item will be shipped.

What is the difference between the four editions?

All four editions contain the same planner structure and features. The difference is the visual aesthetic and cover design.

What is included in the Trial Edition?

The Trial Edition covers December 2026 and January 2027. It is a shorter way to experience the Dear Chapters planning system before choosing the complete edition.

Can I upgrade from the Trial Edition?

Yes. You can upgrade to the Full 2027 Edition for an additional RM39.90.

How will I receive my planner?

Your digital files and download instructions will be provided after your purchase through the checkout platform.

Can I share or resell my planner?

No. Your purchase is for personal use only. The planner and its contents may not be redistributed, resold or shared.
