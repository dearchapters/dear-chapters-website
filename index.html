Is this a physical planner?

No. Dear Chapters planners are digital PDF products. No physical item will be shipped.

How will I receive my planner?

After a successful purchase through our Payhip store, you will receive access to your digital download.

What is the difference between the four editions?

All four Full 2027 Editions contain the same planner pages, sections and features. Only the visual design differs.

What is included in the Trial Edition?

The Trial Edition includes the same core planning experience for December 2026 and January 2027 only.

Can I upgrade after buying the Trial Edition?

Yes. Trial customers can purchase the Full 2027 Edition upgrade for RM44.90. The Trial Edition must have been purchased and paid for first.

Can I use the planner on my tablet?

The planner is a digital PDF designed for use with compatible PDF annotation apps. Please check that your preferred app supports PDF hyperlinks.

Can I share or resell the planner?

No. Your purchase is for personal use only. Copying, sharing, redistributing or reselling the files is not permitted.
